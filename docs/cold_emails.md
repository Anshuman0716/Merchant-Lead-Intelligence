# Cold emails: 20 Tier A leads

Each email follows the same structure, kept under 120 words:
**personalised line from the tool → one pain → one proof point → soft ask.**

Rules I held myself to:
- Only claim what the public signals show ("checks out via Razorpay Magic Checkout"), never
  something I can't see ("your success rate is low").
- Proof points describe product capabilities. **No made-up numbers.** Before sending, swap the
  `[proof]` sentence for a real, citable Cashfree case study or metric.
- Addressed to the buyer title from the ICP. Find the real person on LinkedIn before sending.

Leads were picked from the Tier A list. I skipped very large companies (Freshworks, Postman,
BrowserStack, HighRadius), because those are enterprise accounts rather than SDR targets, and
Chargebee, because it's a billing vendor itself.
